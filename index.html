I am currently an Instructor in Mathematics at Brandeis University. I completed my Ph.D. in 2022 at Queen's University, under the supervision of Francesco Cellarosi. I enjoy studying problems at the interface of probability, and analytic number theory, typically with the aid of methods from homogeneous dynamics. Recently, I have also become interested in studying probabilistic questions in the world of translation surfaces.
