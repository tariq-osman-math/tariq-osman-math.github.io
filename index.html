I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at Universität Zürich working with Claire Burrin. Previously I was an Instructor in Mathematics at Brandeis University. I completed my Ph.D. in 2022 at Queen's University, under the supervision of Francesco Cellarosi. I enjoy studying problems at the intersection of number theory, probability, and dynamical systems. Recently, I have also become interested in studying probabilistic questions in the world of translation surfaces.
